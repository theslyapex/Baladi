Persian bonus-word dictionary

The word list in bonus_words.json is derived from MehdiRaash/persian-words:
https://github.com/MehdiRaash/persian-words

That repository states that its word collection is based on Persian dictionaries, currently Moein, and is distributed under the MIT License. The source JSON was normalized to Persian Yeh/Kaf forms and filtered to 2–6 characters in the Persian alphabet for this game. Current official stage answers missing from the source list are added as manually reviewed entries.
